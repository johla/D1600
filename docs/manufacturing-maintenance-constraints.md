# Manufacturing and maintenance constraints

Provisional constraints for the generic demo:

- all internals fit inside nominal 1.600 m chamber ID;
- minimum hydraulic gap 75 mm unless explicitly reviewed;
- minimum vacuum/inspection access 400 mm;
- no blind sediment pocket inaccessible to suction cleaning;
- removable insert sections must fit through the intended access opening;
- no fine screens or disposable media;
- no powered/moving mechanisms;
- simple plate/formed/rotationally symmetric parts are preferred;
- geometry complexity is an optimization cost, not free performance.

MFT must replace these with actual fabrication and service rules before product conclusions.
