# C2 hydraulic validation plan — required as a C1 artifact

## Purpose

Calibrate and falsify CFD rankings and absolute particle capture for the baseline and 2–3 selected D1600 candidates.

## Proposed campaign

- transparent scale model or full-scale D1600 rig, with similarity limitations documented;
- controlled recirculating water flow and calibrated flowmeter;
- upstream/downstream water levels for head loss;
- known particle feed with representative 40/63/100/250 µm classes;
- time-synchronized inlet/outlet sampling and retained-mass recovery;
- PIV/dye/residence-time observations where practical;
- clean, partly filled and overload conditions;
- repeated runs and mass closure;
- blind CFD prediction before selected tests where possible.

The final plan must resolve scale, similarity, feed uniformity, sampling bias, particle recovery and uncertainty.
